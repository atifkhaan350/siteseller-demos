Our Story

Tradition, served warm.

At Côte Rôtie, every plate begins with French tradition and ends with a Karachi welcome. Our chefs work with locally sourced produce and classic techniques — slow braises, delicate sauces, and pastries baked fresh every morning.

Whether it's an intimate dinner for two or a celebration with family, our candlelit dining room in Block 8, Clifton is ready for you — seven days a week.
